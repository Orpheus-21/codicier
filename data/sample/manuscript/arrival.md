The boat came in on the morning tide. Tomas stepped onto the pier and did not look at the lamp.

He had promised himself that much on the crossing: that he would come back, that he would do what he came to do, and that he would not look up at the tower as if he were still nine years old and waiting for someone to wave.

A woman stood at the end of the pier with a log book under her arm. She was looking at him the way the sea looks at a rock.
