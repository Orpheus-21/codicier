By nine the sea had turned white at the rocks. The radio crackled, went silent, and crackled again.

“Calling the light at Skarv,” a voice said. “This is the *Margit*, two miles out, in need of a bearing.”

Elin knew the name of the ship. Her father had painted it on a board above the door, then painted over it, the winter she was ten.

She gave the bearing. Her voice did not shake. She wrote the name in the log, and under it, after a long moment, a question mark.
